# Recommended business improvements

These are proposed additions, not invented claims added to the website. Priorities reflect a local solar installer serving Jaipur.

## Before launch

| Priority | Improvement                                              | Business value / evidence needed                                                                                   |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1        | Verify 500+ customers, 1K+ panels and 5+ years           | These came from the supplied design. Publish only figures supported by business records.                           |
| 1        | Confirm address, legal trading identity and service area | Keep website, invoices and Google Business Profile consistent. Phone, email and weekday hours are already updated. |
| 1        | Confirm actual subsidy-assistance capability             | Show registration/empanelment only if current and verifiable. Avoid guarantees about approval or savings.          |
| 1        | Set a real response target and lead owner                | Prevent inquiries from remaining unread; only publish a response-time promise the team can meet.                   |

Google recommends complete, accurate Business Profile information for local visibility. Use the same name, phone, address and hours across the site and your verified profile. [Google guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en).

## Next additions with the highest value

1. **Real Jaipur projects.** Add 4–8 owner-approved project cards with locality, system capacity, installation date, equipment and actual photographs. Where savings or generation are shown, state the measurement period and relevant assumptions.
2. **Verified reviews and references.** Link to the actual Google Business Profile and request reviews from real customers. Obtain permission for named testimonials; avoid fabricated ratings or review schema.
3. **A simple solar journey.** Explain inquiry → electricity-use review → roof survey → itemized proposal → installation → commissioning/net-metering assistance → support. Confirm which steps the business actually handles.
4. **Subsidy guidance.** Add an FAQ linking to the official PM Surya Ghar portal and current scheme material. Explain residential eligibility and registered-vendor requirements without presenting a subsidy as guaranteed. [MNRE rooftop programme resources](https://mnre.gov.in/en/grid-connected-solar-rooftop-programme/) and [official consumer guidance](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/feb/doc2025212499601.pdf).
5. **Clear equipment and service coverage.** Show actual brands offered, panel/inverter specifications, manufacturer warranty documents, workmanship coverage, AMC inclusions/exclusions and escalation contacts.
6. **Useful customer FAQs.** Cover roof suitability, shading, cleaning, installation disruption, net-metering, and the difference between grid-connected generation and a system designed for backup. Have the installer verify technical answers against the specific equipment offered.
7. **Better lead qualification without a long form.** Later add optional locality/PIN code, property type and monthly bill range. Keep the first contact simple; collect electricity bills or other documents through a secure follow-up process when needed.
8. **WhatsApp Business, if this number is enabled.** Add a clearly labelled customer-initiated chat link once the owner confirms the number is on WhatsApp Business. Do not auto-subscribe inquiries to marketing.
9. **A Hindi version.** Translate the key service, inquiry, subsidy and support information after reviewing terminology with the business.
10. **A staff lead dashboard.** Add authenticated assignment, notes, next-call dates, overdue reminders and restricted exports when email and D1 console become cumbersome.

## Later, after reliable lead handling

Consider a savings estimator only after defining tariffs, roof assumptions, system losses, financing assumptions and maintenance costs. Show ranges and assumptions, and label the result as an estimate requiring a site assessment.

Measure completed inquiries, calls, survey appointments, quotes and signed projects. Add analytics only with an appropriate privacy notice and consent approach. The current website contains no marketing trackers.

Create substantive service and locality pages only where the business actually operates. Avoid mass-produced location pages with repeated text. Publish maintenance advice and project evidence that answers actual customer questions.
